# Change Manifest: C10 maintained-book mid

## Summary
Runtime portfolio marks, execution stale checks, and persisted top-of-book snapshots now consume the already-maintained OKX order book instead of indexing raw WebSocket deltas.

## Business rule(s) affected
R1.7.

## Trigger area(s) (DOC_IMPACT_MATRIX)
A2 portfolio/execution and A5 backtesting direct-caller compatibility.

## Files changed
- `src/okx_quant/engine.py` — pass `MarketDataHandler.books[inst_id]` to consumers and persist its top levels. This is the relocated owner of the task's stale `src/okx_quant/api/engine.py` path.
- `src/okx_quant/portfolio/portfolio_manager.py` — mark positions from `OkxBook.mid()`.
- `src/okx_quant/execution/execution_handler.py` — update stale-check mids from `OkxBook.mid()`.
- `backtesting/replay.py` — pass its already-maintained book to the two changed consumer signatures; no replay behavior or book construction changed.
- `tests/unit/test_execution_flow.py`, `tests/unit/test_wsc_trade_safety.py` — adapt the existing call and guard deep/removal deltas.
- `docs/DOMAIN_RULES.md`, `docs/INVARIANTS.md`, `docs/FAILURE_MODES.md` — add R1.7/I71/F74.
- `docs/DATA_FLOW.md` — record that live and replay consumers share the sequence-maintained book.

## Behavior delta
- Before: consumers indexed the first level of a raw `books` delta, and one-sided deltas were not persisted.
- After: all three consumers use the sequence-maintained book's actual best bid/ask; one-sided/deletion deltas persist the current maintained top.
- Money/risk impact: prevents false mark-to-market equity and stale-order decisions from deep or removed levels.
